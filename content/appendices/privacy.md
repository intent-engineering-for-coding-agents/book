# Privacy

This site uses GoatCounter to count page views and understand which parts of the book readers visit. The site does not use advertising trackers or set analytics cookies.

GoatCounter receives the information a browser sends with a page request, including the requested page, referrer, browser information, and an IP address. GoatCounter removes identifying parts of the IP address before storing a salted hash. The site owner sees aggregate traffic reports rather than profiles of individual readers.

The analytics data is processed by GoatCounter under its [privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy). GitHub Pages also processes requests needed to serve this site under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The site has no accounts, contact forms, advertising, or personalized tracking. Feedback and contributions go through GitHub, where GitHub's own privacy terms apply.

Questions about this notice can be raised through the book's [feedback channels](/appendices/feedback).
